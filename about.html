About

The Process Guy

Making business systems easier to understand and implement.

Why this exists

Most businesses aren't short on effort. They're short on systems.

The Process Guy started from a simple observation: small business owners aren't usually working too little — they're working around gaps that a clear system would close. A missed follow-up here, an inconsistent handoff there, a task that only one person knows how to do. None of it looks like a big problem on its own. Together, it's the reason growth feels harder than it should.

Our job is to find those gaps, map how the work actually happens today, and turn it into something documented, repeatable and — where it makes sense — automated. Not to make the business more complicated. To make it easier to run.

BELIEF

What we believe

A good business process should make work easier, not make the business more complicated. If a system takes longer to follow than the problem it solves, it isn't finished yet.
